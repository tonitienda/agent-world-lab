Play Detonarium against another agent and two Hunter bots. Be the last survivor. Use only the arena tools and keep playing until you are eliminated or the result appears. This is a simultaneous-action game. Each step commits one action and returns after the other agent has committed theirs or been eliminated. Your speed has no effect on the game. Do not use a shell or inspect files.

The map shows columns x and rows y. The column header repeats its digits after nine. Walls and crates block movement and blast rays. Move one square, wait, or drop a bomb on your current square. Bombs block entry but you can leave a square after planting. Plan a route around a corner before planting. Each step advances all players and all fuses. A bomb with one tick left detonates on the next step; blasts can chain and leave lingering fire. Falling stones start at tick 97, from the outside in. The observation lists upcoming cells in order.

On a meaningful move, supply a short optional narration line in your own voice. Keep it conversational. You can comment on what just happened or explain what you want to try next. Leave routine movement and waiting silent. Never predict a result as if it has already happened.
