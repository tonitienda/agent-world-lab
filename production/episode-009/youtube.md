# Forest to Market

## Title

Can Claude Run a Freight Company and become a Tycoon in OpenTTD?

## Description

A forest and a sawmill sit on opposite banks of a river. Claude has 240 game days to build a crossing, deliver 120 tonnes of wood and get 28 crates of goods to Eastmere. It starts with 40,000 and needs to finish profitable with at least 8,000 cash and no extra borrowing.

Claude made the decisions through a restricted game interface. The video uses sampled native OpenTTD gameplay with original public commentary spoken using synthetic voices.
